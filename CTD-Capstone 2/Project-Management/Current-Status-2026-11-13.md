## Working direction: MetamorphUs
- **Concept:** strangers restore a dying digital ecosystem together using physical controllers; when they hold every element in balance, the world transforms into a glowing butterfly. Framed in our proposal as strangers “becoming Mother Earth together.”
- **Core rules:** strangers must cooperate and one person can’t succeed alone; physical controllers drive a digital ecosystem on a screen.
- **Group size:** 4 to 5 controllers (professor suggested about 6).
- **Elements:** sunlight, water, wind, heat or temperature, nutrients (exact set open).
- **Still open (shown as `[X]` on slides):** controller form, how to prevent solo play (floor dots, range lock, camera, etc.), environmental events, display (screen or projection), software (p5.js, Unity, TouchDesigner…).
