## Expert feedback

### Professor feedback, Nov 6, 2026 (from Kaitlyn's notes)
- Split the work into Sensing and Display.
- Push toward groups of strangers (about 6) acting together; the payoff should be hard and worth it.
- Feasibility should focus on sensing: several inputs weighed together, simple output; ask testers "this would be better if…".
- Study Pearl Street buskers: much of the act is building and holding a crowd.

## Interviews

### Interview, [who / role], [date]
What they said (paraphrased from our notes):
- The way a person controls their element should be relevant to that element and distinct from the other elements' controls.
- It would be cool if reaching your element's goal were a little difficult.

Our interpretation (ideas to test, not decisions):
- Themed controls per element (crank for sun, pump for water) over identical knobs.
- Tune difficulty so each element takes real effort to keep in its healthy range, without making it frustrating.

### Interview 2, [role], [date] (full answers in the team's Feasibility Tests doc)
What they said (from the team's notes):
- Moved a fallen tree out of the road with strangers; everyone worked together without saying anything; felt empowered and connected.
- Running a club where everyone had to do their part felt a little stressful: giving up control and depending on others.
- Gut reaction: it requires a lot of communication.
- Would feel very comfortable cooperating with strangers if there is a shared goal people choose to pursue.
- With no instructions, imagined a lever: up is a lot, down is a little, the sweet spot is in the middle.
- Droughts or storms would add a lot and invoke more conversation and collaboration.
- Floor dots should show foot placement and light up when stepped on.
- The butterfly would feel earned if it were difficult; wants noise, light, and maybe the controller vibrates.
- If they could change one thing: make it scientifically sound, with realistic events.

Our interpretation (ideas to test, not decisions): see Controller Research V2 (https://claude.ai/artifact/GRzcMg8LhHLAHiU3sHW2WC) and `Ideation/Design-Ideas-To-Explore.md`.
