How MetamorphUs works

From a stranger’s hands to a glowing world, and back again

3

Arduino

Reads every sensor and sends each element’s level

USB + Web Serial (wireless option: ESP32)

4

Computer

Runs the balance rules, the hold timer and the no-playing-alone rule

Software: [X] (p5.js in our tests)

5

Screen or projection

The ecosystem: barren → glowing forest → butterfly

Display: [X]

Light, sound or vibration back on the controllers (optional)

People see the world react, call out to each other, and adjust together

How MetamorphUs works: each controller sends its element’s level to the computer, which checks the balance rules and grows the world. [X] marks choices the team has not made yet.
