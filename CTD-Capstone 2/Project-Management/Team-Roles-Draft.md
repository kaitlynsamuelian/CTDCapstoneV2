# Team roles: draft (Nov 13, 2026)

**Status: draft proposed by Kaitlyn. Confirm with Caitlin and Austin, then record in `Decision-Log.md`.**

## Kaitlyn: project lead + controls and code
- Project direction, schedule, milestones and team coordination
- Circuits, sensors and Arduino programming: the code that reads the controllers
- The game rules in code: balance, timing, and the no-playing-alone rule
- Making the full system work end to end

## Caitlin: physical experience
- Controller design: how each element is controlled, its form and materials
- Fabrication and assembly of the controllers and the installation
- Building the electronics into the controllers so they survive hours of use
- Installation layout, look and setup in the space
- Leading playtests and user research

## Austin: digital world
- Visual design of the ecosystem: barren, balanced and blooming states
- 2D or 3D assets and animation, including the butterfly transformation
- How the world visibly responds to each element on screen
- Display setup (screen or projection) and sound (if used)
- Tool-neutral: works in whatever the team chooses (p5.js, Unity, TouchDesigner, Blender for assets, and so on)

## Handoffs
- **Kaitlyn ↔ Caitlin:** Kaitlyn decides what gets wired where; Caitlin makes it physically solid inside each controller.
- **Kaitlyn ↔ Austin:** agree on the values the controllers send (e.g. sun 0–100, water 0–100); Kaitlyn delivers them, Austin makes the world react.

## Shared by everyone
Research, the proposal document, the presentation and testing. Leads: playtests (Caitlin), schedule (Kaitlyn), visual documentation (Austin).

## Earlier draft (not adopted)
An earlier split had Caitlin as lead and electronics, Kaitlyn on fabrication, and Austin on Blender-to-Unity integration. Kaitlyn found too much overlap, and Blender-to-Unity assumed a tool choice not yet made.
